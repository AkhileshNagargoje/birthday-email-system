      Import a file
      

        Takes a .csv or .xlsx — a Google Forms
        export works as-is. Column headings are matched loosely, so
        “Full Name”, “Email Address”, “Date of Birth”, “DOB” and similar are all
        understood, in any order.
      

      

        People already on the list are matched by email and updated,
        never duplicated. Rows with a missing name, a bad address or an
        unreadable date are skipped and listed for you — nothing is guessed at.
