About

I speak fluent model.

I specialize in prompt architecture, evaluation design, and agentic workflows for teams building serious products on top of large language models. My work sits at the intersection of linguistics, systems thinking, and product judgment.

Before prompt engineering had a name, I was writing rule-based dialogue trees and fine-tuning small transformers. Today I help teams get more out of frontier models — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama — without a single line of retraining.

I care most about reliability under distribution shift: prompts that don't just demo well, but hold up against messy real-world input, adversarial users, and edge cases nobody thought to test.

Prompt Architecture

Structured, versioned prompt systems with clear roles, constraints, and few-shot scaffolding built for maintainability.

Evaluation & Testing
